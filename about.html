Phillip Haberman

I'm a data analyst with five years of experience, moving into analytics engineering. I build dbt models and dashboards for a sports bar in Shanghai, and this site is where I show that work.

Day to day I model sales, inventory, labor, and marketing data in dbt, maintain dashboards in Metabase, and answer business questions for HR, Marketing, Operations, and Finance. Over the last 5 years I've helped lower operational costs, saved the team countless hours by automating reports, and built up the company's analytics capacity.

Before data, I studied applied linguistics at Shanghai Jiaotong University (a global top 50 university). I still think about how it shapes the way you work, such as clear definitions, ambiguity in metrics, or teaching others. I've spent the majority of my career working in multicultural environments including the US, Shanghai, and Latin America, so I'm used to working across time zones and explaining things to different audiences.

Tools

Day to day: dbt, SQL, Python, Dagster and Metabase. Warehouses I've shipped on: BigQuery, MotherDuck and MySQL. I also build small internal apps with FastAPI.
